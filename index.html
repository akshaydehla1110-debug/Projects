The pipeline

From raw audio to final cut — in one flow.

Every stage is transparent and real: actual decoding, actual signal analysis, actual frame-by-frame rendering and an actual downloadable file. No mock-ups.

01

Upload song

Drop an MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A or AAC file. It's decoded locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

02

Analyze audio

Exact duration, tempo (BPM), energy curve, dynamics and section structure are extracted from the real waveform.

03

Generate & sync

Scene generators build enough visual sequences to cover the full track, cut on bars and transitions blended between them.

04

Render & download

Real-time capture muxes picture and sound into one file — preview it, then download the finished music video.

Questions

Good to know before you render

Does the generated video really match my song's length?

Yes. The planner reads the exact decoded duration of your file, then generates enough scenes to cover the entire track end-to-end. The recorder stops when the audio ends, so the exported video duration equals the song duration.

Do I need an API key or an account?

No. The complete workflow — analysis, scene generation, transitions, captions and export — runs locally with free, standards-based browser technology. Optional external render nodes are entirely up to you.

Which formats can I upload and download?

Upload MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A or AAC. Output is MP4 where your browser supports H.264 recording, otherwise WebM (VP9/VP8) — the studio shows the exact codec used before you download.

Why does rendering take about as long as the song?

Because frames and audio are captured together in real time, which guarantees perfect audio/video sync and a real, honest progress bar. For faster-than-realtime rendering you can connect a local FFmpeg or GPU worker through the engine panel.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

Never. The file is decoded in memory on your device and the finished video is created locally. Closing the tab clears everything.
